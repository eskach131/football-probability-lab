What the words mean

Confidence
The chance the prediction comes true. 80% means about 4 in 5 similar predictions were right in the past, so 1 in 5 still loses.
Model
The app's own estimate, worked out from each team's goals, shots and recent results.
Bookmaker
The chance implied by the betting odds after removing the bookmaker's margin. On past matches this has been more accurate than the model.
Past picks like this
How often the app's earlier predictions of the same kind and confidence came true.
Fair odds and edge
Fair odds are 1 ÷ confidence. Edge is how much better the bookmaker's price is than that. A positive edge is rare and often just noise.
Expected goals
How many goals the app expects each side to score on average. It's a prediction, not shot-by-shot xG.
Double chance
"Team or draw" wins if that team wins or the match is drawn.
Draw-no-bet
Wins if the team wins. If it's a draw, the stake is returned.

Where the data comes from

  • ESPN public feeds (site.api.espn.com and sports.core.api.espn.com): fixtures, scores, shots, corners, and DraftKings odds for about 36 leagues. They're unofficial and free, and ESPN could change or limit them at any time.
  • GitHub: martj42/international_results: national-team results and fixtures.
  • GitHub: openfootball/football.json: a backup used only if ESPN can't be reached.

How a prediction is made

  1. Team ratings. Every team gets an attack and a defence rating from its results, with recent games counting more. Goals are mixed with shots, which smooths out lucky and unlucky scorelines.
  2. Tuning. For each league the app tries several settings, plus a second rating system (Elo), and keeps whatever predicted past scores best.
  3. Scores. The ratings give each side's expected goals. From those the app works out the chance of every scoreline, and from that every type of prediction.
  4. Bookmaker blend. Where odds exist, they're mixed in at the bookmaker weight. On past matches the bookmaker alone did best, so that weight is usually 100%.
  5. Reality check. Each kind of prediction is corrected to match how often it actually came true over the past year.
  6. Corners. Teams have separate corner ratings. Corner predictions come from the model alone and are less reliable than goal predictions.

Limits

  • Football is unpredictable. Even bookmakers pick the winner only about half the time. High-confidence picks are mostly safe lines such as "over 0.5 goals" or a strong favourite not losing.
  • Injuries, line-ups and motivation reach the app only through the bookmaker's odds.
  • Several picks from the same match win or lose together. Combining picks into one bet multiplies the risk: four 80% picks all come in only about 41% of the time.
  • This is for analysis and entertainment. It isn't financial advice.
